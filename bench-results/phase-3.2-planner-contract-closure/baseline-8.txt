## Two root causes, not one

### A. Contract assignment (tests 02, 04, 05, 11, 42)

`requirement_intent("Build a multi-tier ecommerce store …")` matched the bare
word **build** and classified the user's *product* request as a BUILD-file
requirement. No explicit or requested file of that role existed, and the
CONFIG/DOC/BUILD fallback only attached to an already-assigned SOURCE (none
existed at that point), so `REQ-001` got no target and the ambiguity blocked
dispatch.

The same wording affects CONFIG/DOC/BUILD in any workspace with no matching
file.

### B. Requirement extraction + evidence (tests 26, 38, 40)

The requirement extractor's verb list did not include `fix`, `refactor` or
`clean`, and its sentence-length guard dropped 10-character requests. The
analyzer returned only optional suggestions (CONFIG/TEST), so the contract had
no mandatory requirement. The plan fell back to `src/main_task.py`; verification
then had no requirement evidence (`NOT_TESTED`) and the task verdict was
`FAILED`. Under the non-pytest diagnostic this was masked by the real-provider
budget check (no provider key in test mode); under pytest it surfaced as the
verdict failure.
