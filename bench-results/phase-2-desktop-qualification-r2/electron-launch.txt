## Ctrl/Cmd+L — not proven (environment limitation)

- `WScript.Shell.AppActivate(pid)` and by title both returned **False**.
- `SetForegroundWindow(MainWindowHandle)` succeeded (foreground == target), but
  `SendKeys("^l")` did not open the pane; a **control** chord `Ctrl+Shift+P`
  also did nothing through the same path, so OS input injection is not reaching
  the app in this session.
- CDP `Control+l` also did not open the pane; the command palette
  `Crusor: Open Agent` did.
- The keybinding is registered (`KeyMod.CtrlCmd | KeyCode.KeyL`,
  `WorkbenchContrib`); its behaviour could not be confirmed through a genuine
  input path here. Recorded as NOT PROVEN, not as a confirmed defect.
