The single sentence: every one of the five keeps the fixed preamble small (0–2.2k tokens, or 5.4k at Codex's largest) and spends its context on ranked structure; we keep 13,660 tokens of preamble and spend the rest on unranked content. That is the whole cost gap, and it is a design choice, not a model limitation.
